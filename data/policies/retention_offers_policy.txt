Retention Offers Policy. Retention offers are made only to subscribers whose churn risk band is High or who have requested closure. A High risk subscriber with a tenure of 12 months or more is eligible for a loyalty discount of 15 percent on the next three recharges. A High risk subscriber with a tenure below 12 months is eligible for a free add-on pack for 2 months. A High risk subscriber with 3 or more failed recharges in the last six months receives an auto-renew setup call and a 7 day grace extension instead of a discount. Only one retention offer can be active on a subscriber at a time, and an offer cannot be combined with a coupon. Offers are approved by the retention desk and must be logged with the subscriber ID, the offer type and the reason. A subscriber who accepted an offer in the last 180 days is not eligible for another one.
