Plan Change Policy. A subscriber may upgrade the plan at any time and the new plan starts immediately; the unused balance of the old plan is adjusted against the new price. A downgrade takes effect from the next billing cycle and can be requested only once in every 90 days. Add-on packs cost INR 49 per month each and can be added or removed at the start of a cycle. A subscriber can hold a maximum of 3 add-on packs at a time. Moving from a standard definition plan to an HD plan needs an HD-capable set top box; the box swap is free for Premium plans and costs INR 250 for all other plans. Plan changes requested after the 25th of the month are applied from the cycle after next. Regional language packs can be paused for up to 60 days once a year.
