Technical Support Policy. Signal loss and pixelation complaints are first handled by a remote refresh of the set top box, attempted within 2 hours of the call. If the problem continues, a technician visit is scheduled within 48 hours, or within 24 hours for Premium and Premium Plus subscribers. The first technician visit in a cycle is free; each additional visit in the same cycle costs INR 150 unless the fault is on the company side. If a fault on the company side lasts longer than 72 hours, the subscriber is credited for the lost days. A replacement remote costs INR 120 and a replacement cable costs INR 90. Complaints about app or channel buffering are logged as app issues and answered within 1 working day.
