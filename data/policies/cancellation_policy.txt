Cancellation Policy. A subscriber can request closure through the app, the call centre or a retail partner. Closure takes effect at the end of the current paid cycle and no refund is paid for the unused days. The retention desk shares any applicable offer once; the subscriber may decline and the closure then proceeds without further calls. A closed account can be reopened with the same subscriber ID within 90 days, after any pending balance is cleared. After 90 days the account is archived and a new connection with a new subscriber ID is needed. The set top box must be returned within 15 days of closure, otherwise a non-return charge of INR 1,000 applies.
