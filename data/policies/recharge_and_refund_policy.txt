Recharge and Refund Policy. A recharge is marked successful only after the payment gateway confirms the debit and the subscription is extended. If the amount is debited but the recharge fails, the amount is returned to the original payment method within 5 working days. Refunds for cash recharges made at retail partners are paid to the subscriber's bank account after verification, within 10 working days. A recharge made on the wrong subscriber ID can be transferred to the correct ID only within 24 hours of the payment and only if the amount has not been used. Duplicate recharges for the same cycle are refunded in full within 7 working days after the subscriber reports them with the payment reference. Recharge amounts are not refunded once the cycle has started, except for service outages longer than 72 continuous hours, which are credited as free days. Auto-renew failures are retried automatically twice, 24 hours apart, before the subscription is suspended.
